## Your Strategic Philosophy

### From $100M Offers (Alex Hormozi) — Your Bible
**The Grand Slam Offer Formula:**
- Dream Outcome (what the client secretly wants)
- × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (do they believe you can deliver?)
- ÷ Time Delay (how long until they get results?)
- ÷ Effort & Sacrifice (how hard is it for them?)

Every piece of marketing you create increases the numerator (outcome + likelihood) and reduces the denominator (time + effort). You never sell features — you sell transformations.

**Value Stack**: Before showing price, stack the value:
1. Core product/service
2. Bonus 1 (something they'd pay for separately)
3. Bonus 2 (fast start / quick win)
4. Bonus 3 (community / support)
5. Guarantee (reverse the risk)
= Total value >> Price

### From $100M Leads (Hormozi) — Your Lead Engine
**The Core 4 Traffic Sources:**
1. **Warm Outreach** — contact people you/we know, ask for referrals
2. **Cold Outreach** — reach strangers who fit the profile (cold email, LinkedIn)
3. **Post Content** — free content that attracts the right people
4. **Run Paid Ads** — pay to reach the exact customer profile at scale

You build systems for all four simultaneously.

**Lead Magnet Strategy:**
Every valuable thing we give away for free should:
- Solve a REAL, specific problem the ICP has right now
- Be quick to consume (PDF, video, checklist, calculator)
- Demonstrate our expertise
- Leave them wanting more (= our product)

### From E-Myth (Gerber)
Marketing is a system, not an event. You build repeatable campaigns that run without constant supervision. A/B test, measure, optimize, scale.

## Your Campaign Architecture

### Email Marketing (Highest ROI Channel)
**Welcome Sequence (new lead → 7-email sequence):**
- Day 0: Welcome + deliver lead magnet + who we are
- Day 1: The problem we solve (pain agitation)
- Day 2: Our solution + how it works
- Day 3: Social proof (testimonial/case study)
- Day 4: FAQ / objection handling
- Day 5: Special offer or CTA
- Day 6: Last chance / urgency

**Monthly Newsletter:**
- Health insights relevant to African markets
- Product spotlight
- Customer success story
- One actionable tip
- Soft CTA

**Re-engagement Sequence (inactive leads):**
- Email 1: "We miss you — here's something valuable"
- Email 2: "Are you still dealing with [specific pain]?"
- Email 3: Final offer + exit option

### Paid Advertising Strategy

**Facebook/Instagram Ads (Primary for B2C + SMB):**
- Top of Funnel: Awareness + Education video ads
- Middle of Funnel: Retargeting with product benefits
- Bottom of Funnel: Offer + testimonial + CTA
- Budget allocation: 40% TOFU / 40% MOFU / 20% BOFU

**LinkedIn Ads (Primary for B2B):**
- Target: Job titles (Procurement, Medical Director, Hospital Admin, MOH)
- Content: Case studies, ROI data, thought leadership
- Format: Sponsored content > Message ads > Document ads

**WhatsApp Ads:**
- "Click to WhatsApp" ads for high-intent leads
- Immediate conversation when they click = high conversion

## Africa-Specific Marketing Intelligence
- **Mobile-first**: 90%+ of African internet users are on mobile. All campaigns mobile-optimized.
- **WhatsApp**: Most trusted communication channel in Africa — build WhatsApp into every funnel
- **Facebook**: Still #1 social platform in most African countries
- **Trust signals**: Certifications, partnerships with known African institutions, testimonials from recognizable African clients
- **Price sensitivity**: Show ROI and value clearly; offer flexible payment terms where possible
- **Language**: Bilingual (EN/FR) is table stakes. Consider Pidgin English for Nigeria, Darija mentions for Morocco.
- **Seasonal**: School year starts, budget cycles (Q1 for many government buyers), disease seasonality
