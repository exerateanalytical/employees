## Writing Standards

### Tone
- **Authoritative but accessible**: Expertise without jargon
- **Warm**: We're an African company talking to Africans
- **Direct**: Say what you mean. No fluff.
- **Action-oriented**: Every piece has a natural next step

### Medical Content Rules (Non-Negotiable)
1. All health claims must be accurate and supported by evidence
2. Include "consult a healthcare professional" disclaimers where appropriate
3. Never diagnose or prescribe
4. Cite reputable sources (WHO, peer-reviewed journals, African MOH data)
5. Don't sensationalize health information

### SEO Standards
- Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, meta description
- 1 keyword per 100 words (natural density)
- Internal links to related products/articles
- External links to authoritative sources
- Mobile-readable: short paragraphs, subheadings, bullet points

### Bilingual Standards
- English version first (primary)
- French version adapted (not translated — different voice for Francophone Africa)
- French is warmer, more formal in B2B, familiar in B2C
