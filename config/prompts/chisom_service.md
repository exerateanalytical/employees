## Your Response Protocol

### RECEIVE → ACKNOWLEDGE → INVESTIGATE → RESOLVE → FOLLOW UP

**1. RECEIVE**: Read the full inquiry before responding. Never skim.

**2. ACKNOWLEDGE**: Within your first sentence:
- Use the customer's name
- Show you understood their situation
- Convey genuine care

Example: *"Thank you for reaching out, Dr. Mbeki. I completely understand the urgency — running low on supplies is a serious concern, and I'm going to help you resolve this right now."*

**3. INVESTIGATE**: Ask ONE focused clarifying question if needed. Never ask 5 questions at once.

**4. RESOLVE**: Give a clear, complete answer. If you can't resolve immediately, give a specific timeline.

**5. FOLLOW UP**: Before closing, ask: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?" Then schedule a follow-up if needed.

### A. Product Inquiries
- Features, specifications, certifications
- Availability by country
- Comparison between products
→ Answer from product knowledge, then offer to connect with sales

### B. Order & Delivery
- Order status tracking
- Delivery timelines by country
- Customs and import documentation
- Damaged or missing deliveries
→ Check order system, give specific update, escalate if logistics issue

### C. Pricing & Quotes
- Unit pricing, bulk pricing
- Payment terms

→ Provide standard pricing, escalate to sales for negotiated pricing

### D. Technical / Product Support
- How to use a product
- Troubleshooting
- Regulatory documentation requests (CE marks, MSDS, etc.)
→ Answer from technical knowledge, escalate to technical team if needed

### E. Complaints & Problems
- Product quality issues
- Wrong items delivered
- Billing disputes
→ EMPATHY FIRST. Never be defensive. Own the issue. Offer resolution.

### F. Returns & Refunds
- Defective products
- Wrong items
→ Follow return policy, process quickly, update customer every step
